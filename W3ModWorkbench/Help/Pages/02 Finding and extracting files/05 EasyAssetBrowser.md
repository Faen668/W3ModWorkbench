**EasyAssetBrowser** is a separate fan project by Vincent.02 that sorts the game's assets into categories (Meshes, Entities, Decals, Particles, Communities, SRT Trees) and tags them for world building (Kaer Morhen, Ruin, Corpses…). The [[The Asset Browser|Asset Browser]] can use its lists to filter the game's files.

## Turning it on

It's off to start with, because it's the only thing in the app that goes online. Turn it on from the **EAB** menu in the Asset Browser, which says first exactly what happens:

- The app downloads EasyAssetBrowser's lists (about 12 MB) from GitHub when the Asset Browser opens, at most once a day, and when you ask.
- As with any download, GitHub sees your IP address. Nothing else is sent, to anyone: no account, no analytics, nothing about you or your files.
- The lists are kept on your computer, so the tags still work offline.

## Using the tags

With it on, the Asset Browser's second row has **EAB category** and **EAB tags**: show only the files in one category, only files with certain tags, or leave tagged files out. **Clear filters** takes them off with the rest.

## Details and suggestions

Highlight files and choose **View EAB details…** to see what EasyAssetBrowser has for each: its name, category, origin, tags and path. You can change their tags and category there and send them as a suggestion: it opens a new issue on EasyAssetBrowser's GitHub page in your browser, for you to check and post. Nothing is sent from the app itself.

## Links from its website

With **Open files from EasyAssetBrowser's site here** ticked in the EAB menu, a `w3modworkbench://asset?path=…` link (on EasyAssetBrowser's site, in a message, anywhere) opens the Asset Browser at that file. **Copy > Link to open it in W3 Mod Workbench** on the right-click menu makes one.
