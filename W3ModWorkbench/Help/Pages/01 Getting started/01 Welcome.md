W3 Mod Workbench is a toolbox for modding **The Witcher 3**. It opens the game's files and other people's mods so you can look around inside them, and it gives you everything you need to make a mod of your own: a place to keep it, a script editor, viewers for the game's meshes, scenes and sounds, and a button that builds the finished mod.

You don't need REDkit or any other tool to use it, though it works happily alongside them.

## The ideas behind it

- **The game's files are never changed.** Everything you open from the game, its DLC or someone else's mod is read-only. When you want to change one, the app copies it into your own project first.
- **Your mod lives in a project.** A project is a folder of your own with your scripts and your copies of game files in it. One click builds it into a mod the game loads. See [[Projects]].
- **Everything is opened as the game opens it.** A mesh finds its textures, a character its clothes and animations, a scene its voices, the same way the game would.
