Double-click a `.journal` file to open the journal viewer with its entry picked. One window shows the whole journal, as REDkit's journal editor does: every journal file of the game, the archives you have open and your project, read together.

![The journal, with a Blood and Wine quest picked](journal-viewer.jpg)

## The tree

- **Quests**: the quest groups (Prologue, each chapter, side quests, each DLC's), each quest with its descriptions, phases, **objectives** and **map pins**
- **Characters**, **Bestiary**, **Glossary**, **Tutorial**, **Places** and the **Storybook**

Each kind of entry has its own icon, and resting on one shows its title in words.

## An entry

Click one to see its texts in words (title, name, description, a quest's objectives), its kind, its **unique script tag** and its file, then every property it has.

- **Copy the script tag** copies the tag scripts find it by.
- **Find** ({{Ctrl+F}}) lists the entries whose name, tag or texts have your words.
- **Tools** has REDkit's checks: duplicated script tags, duplicated GUIDs, and entries without a script tag.
