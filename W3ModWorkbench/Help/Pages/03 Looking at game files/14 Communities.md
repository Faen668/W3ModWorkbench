A **community** (`.w2comm`) says who lives somewhere and when. Double-click one to open the community viewer. Its type is at the top, with how many entries, story phases and timetables it has.

![Triss's community: where she is in each part of the story](community-viewer.jpg)

## Spawnset

Laid out as REDkit's community editor lays it out: each entry with a row for each of its entities (the template spawned, its appearances and tags) and each of its **story phases** (the quest tags it spawns in, then each spawn time: when, how many, how soon it respawns), with the timetable it keeps and where it spawns.

Click a row to see its entry's, entity's, story phase's and spawn time's properties. Double-click it (or **Open the template**) to open the entity, and **Its timetable** goes to the phase's timetable.

## Timetables

Each timetable by name: at each time of day, the layer whose action points it uses, and each kind of action there with its weight and tags.

The search at the top ({{Ctrl+F}}) narrows both lists to what has your words.
